## Primary-source extraction requirement

Before bioRxiv submission, every physical intervention statement must be entered in a dedicated evidence ledger containing:

- paper identifier;
- biological system;
- intervention;
- measured outcome;
- whether the result is direct or inferred;
- exact manuscript claim supported;
- limitations and jurisdiction.

## Traceability rule

Every numerical or mechanistic claim in the final manuscript must resolve to a repository path, claim-ledger row and evidence class. Published experimental evidence must never be merged silently with our model counterfactuals.
