# HGMF‑HG44 — Claim lattice and narrative discipline

## Claim sets

Let S be the set of carriers/cells required by a conjunction claim.

The evidence is

E_S(t)=min_(j in S) E_j(t).

If S grows, the claim becomes stronger and evidence cannot increase.

## Authorized narrative movement

- stronger claim after evidence: conservative but may lose power;
- weaker claim after evidence: not automatically authorized;
- carrier-local report: always labelled carrier-local;
- screening claim: uses a preregistered weighted mean;
- sequential claim: requires a frozen filtration and conditional-factor proof.

## Narrative theorem

Every scientific sentence about a state change must be reconstructible as:

premises in F_(t-1)
+ frozen transition rule
+ admitted event
=> new conformation K_t.

A post hoc explanation that cannot be written in this form is not part of the confirmatory narrative. It may be reported as exploratory reinformation, separately labelled and unable to alter the sealed process.
