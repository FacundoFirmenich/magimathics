# HGMF-HG31 — Literature anchors

## Universal inference / e-values

- Wasserman, Ramdas & Balakrishnan, *Universal Inference* (2020 preprint): split likelihood ratios with finite-sample guarantees for composite models.
- Nguyen et al., *Generalized universal inference on risk minimizers*, JRSS B 88(3), 756–778 (2026): finite-sample/anytime-valid extensions.
- Dunn et al., *Robust universal inference for misspecified models*, Biometrika 113(2) (2026): projection-target inference under misspecification.
- *Gaussian universal likelihood ratio testing*, Biometrika 110(2), 319–337 (2023): finite-sample validity when an upper bound on null maximum likelihood is available.

## Predictive checks

- *Calibrated Model Criticism Using Split Predictive Checks*, JASA (2026): split predictive checks avoid double use and have asymptotic calibration under stated conditions.

## Process tensors

- Pollock et al./review literature summarized in PRX Quantum 2, 030201 (2021): process tensors, MPO representations and bond dimension as effective memory/environment dimension.
- White et al., *Non-Markovian Quantum Process Tomography*, PRX Quantum 3, 020344 (2022): positive Choi representation, affine causality constraints, maximum-likelihood process-tensor tomography.
- *Process trees: ...*, Phys. Rev. X 14, 041018 (2024): universality of process-tensor descriptions when environment dimension is unrestricted.

## Claim boundary

These references support the mathematical/statistical machinery and the breadth of standard non-Markovian models. They do not support a claim that HGMF is physically realized.
