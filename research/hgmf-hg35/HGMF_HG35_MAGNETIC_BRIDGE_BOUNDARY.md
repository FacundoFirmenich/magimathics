# HGMF-HG35 — Magnetic quantitative bridge boundary

The source paper and repository provide:
- AHE hysteresis Vxy(H);
- extracted HEB, coercive fields and AHE amplitudes;
- theoretical CrSBr M/MS vs H curves;
- FM/AFM magnetization profiles Mz/Ms versus spatial coordinate;
- a free-energy phenomenological model supporting metastable CrSBr and interfacial domain formation.

They do not provide a calibrated stochastic forward map

P(Vxy(H) | K_FGT, K_CrSBr, J_int, domain state, ...).

Therefore HG35 authorizes:
- source-specific summary interval null on HEB;
- structural switching-width tests;
- diagnostic training-law fits.

It does not authorize a pointwise micromagnetic Vxy likelihood or a universal-evalue denominator from the source model.
