# HGMF‑UMI‑21 — Carrier-Native Semantic Measure and Scale-Range Extension

## Objective

Replace model-residual witnesses with carrier-native intervention/history measures and extend the independent scale range.

## Gate A — native measure

Define H/D/V directly from:

- within-layer response;
- branch/history separation;
- inter-layer state change;
- interventions or reset responses.

No model-comparison residual may define the measure.

## Gate B — carriers

Use at least two non-isomorphic carriers with at least six independent retained scales each.

## Gate C — finite-size scaling

Use genuinely larger records or repeated hierarchies, not interpolation-based pseudo-resolution.

## Gate D — surrogate tribunal

Retain full shuffle, regime shuffle, block permutation, finite mixtures and hierarchy randomization.

## Gate E — transport

Test equality/equivalence of spectra under a predeclared UMI transport map.

## Gate F — triaxiality

Estimate marginal and joint spectra with an explicit intervention-based vertical witness.

## Gate G — portfolio

- Paper A remains tied to the UMI‑17 experiment.
- Paper B may claim formal multifractal characterization but not empirical universality.
- Paper C receives no bridge unless this gate passes transport and surrogate tribunals.

## Authorized outcomes

- `PASS_NATIVE_EMPIRICAL_MULTIFRACTAL_UMI`
- `PASS_NATIVE_MULTISCALE_ONLY`
- `FAIL_TRANSPORT_INVARIANCE`
- `FAIL_SURROGATE_TRIBUNAL`
- `ABSTAIN_INSUFFICIENT_INDEPENDENT_SCALES`
