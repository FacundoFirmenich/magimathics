# MGM-UMI-REAL-03 — Frozen Methods

## Universal tribunal

For every candidate:

1. freeze the semantic signature;
2. define admissible transformations;
3. enumerate or approximate the minimal relational carrier;
4. substitute components and representations;
5. preserve cardinality while rewiring relations;
6. ablate each critical relation;
7. demonstrate transport to a distinct physical or computational substrate;
8. state the jurisdiction and exclusions.

## Frozen signatures

- **ECC:** recover the encoded message after every single-bit error.
- **Memory:** maintain two bounded persistent states and support controlled set/reset.
- **Homeostasis:** stably attenuate a persistent disturbance by the preregistered factor.
- **Consensus:** prevent two conflicting values from both becoming chosen.

## Independence adjudication

A passing candidate is compared with prior carriers for strict nesting, refinement or equivalence before it is counted as a new UMI class.

## Evidence separation

Exact theorem, exhaustive finite computation, numerical model, published implementation and inference remain separately labelled. Model counterfactuals are never described as empirical measurements.
