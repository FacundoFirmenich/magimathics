# HGMF‑HG35 — Literature anchors

## FGT/CrSBr

Kumar et al., Small 21, e06284 (2025), DOI 10.1002/smll.202506284:
- first loop after preset field shows pronounced exchange bias/asymmetry;
- subsequent loops become symmetric/gradual;
- source mechanism invokes interfacial exchange, competing anisotropies, metastable/canted CrSBr and domain evolution.

The public source repository contains theoretical M/MS curves and spatial FM/AFM profiles but no calibrated Vxy probability model.

## Brown Xe/Vycor

Siderius, Journal of Research of NIST 126, 126037 (2021), DOI 10.6028/jres.126.037:
- digitized Brown scanning histories;
- independent-domain theory historical context;
- successor percolation/network models remain relevant.

Mason, Proc. R. Soc. A 415, 453–486 (1988), DOI 10.1098/rspa.1988.0023:
- cavity/window Bethe-tree model;
- applied directly to Brown Xe/Vycor scanning isotherms at 151 K;
- uses cavity-size distribution, window-size distribution and windows per cavity.

Cimino et al., Colloids Surf. A 437, 76–89 (2013), DOI 10.1016/j.colsurfa.2013.03.025:
- Bethe and 3D-lattice percolation models;
- Vycor z=6.6 and 7.2 from two scans;
- qc=0.18 and 0.16;
- z=6.6 gives good scanning-desorption agreement except the lowest scan;
- measured adsorption is mapped to pore-state fraction through reference isotherms Vs and Vc.
