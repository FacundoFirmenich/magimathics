# HGMF‑HG02 — Multi‑Context Protogeometric Tomography and Observational Saturation

## Objective

Determine how much of a protogeometric code can be reconstructed by combining multiple contextual projections.

## Gate A — joint map

\[
\Phi_{\Lambda_*}(P)=\left(\Phi_\lambda(P)\right)_{\lambda\in\Lambda_*}.
\]

## Gate B — identifiability theorem

Prove

\[
\Phi_{\Lambda_*}\text{ injective}
\iff
\bigcap_{\lambda\in\Lambda_*}\sim_\lambda
=
\Delta_{\mathcal P}.
\]

## Gate C — minimal separating family

Find the smallest context family that separates all admissible states, or prove that none exists.

## Gate D — observational saturation

Define the irreducible residual class

\[
[P]_{\rm sat}=
\bigcap_{\lambda\in\Lambda_{adm}}[P]_{\sim_\lambda}.
\]

## Gate E — hyperaccess

Quantify fibre contraction as the observer is enlarged, and identify when new contexts cease to contribute information.

## Gate F — proto-certainty

Separate:

- reduction of observational indeterminacy;
- transitional identity;
- geometric certainty;
- ontology of the foundation.

## Gate G — quantum boundary

Do not introduce Born, spin or special constants unless they emerge as independent consequences of the identification problem.
