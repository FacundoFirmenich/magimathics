# P2b Editorial Contract

## Manuscript identity

**Working title:** *Minimal Invariant Relational Units in Synthetic and Systems Biology*

**Primary question:** Can biological mechanism units be identified by a frozen relation-first intervention tribunal rather than by structural motif occurrence or phenotype resemblance alone?

**Preprint home:** bioRxiv `Systems Biology`; alternative `Synthetic Biology` if engineered circuits dominate the final version.

## Cases owned by P2b

### Core detailed cases

1. repressilator / sustained oscillation;
2. chemotaxis, internal-model and antithetic robust perfect adaptation;
3. genetic toggle / writable bistable memory;
4. kinetic proofreading;
5. synthetic Turing pattern formation;
6. minimal RAF/autocatalytic closure.

### Ambiguous biological case

7. quorum-sensing competence switch — adjudicated as `ABSTAIN` when multiple minimal carriers remain plausible or intervention evidence is incomplete.

## Cases excluded from the biological centre

- Hamming error correction;
- distributed consensus;
- topological edge transport as a primary case;
- mechanical/electrical homeostasis as a primary case.

These belong to P2. Electronic or physical implementations may appear only as transport controls for a biological mechanism.

## Biological evidential requirement

Every positive case must distinguish:

- graph topology;
- kinetic/physical jurisdiction;
- strict biological semantic signature;
- direct or model-supported intervention;
- matched biological or mechanistic decoy;
- transport across species, implementation or biochemical substrate;
- minimality status;
- evidence class.

## Primary claim

The contribution is not a new biological motif catalogue. It is a method for adjudicating when a biological relation class deserves treatment as a unit and for distinguishing mechanism identity from phenotype resemblance.

- references to P2 only after stable public identifiers exist.
