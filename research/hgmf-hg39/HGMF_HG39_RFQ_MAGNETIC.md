# HGMF‑HG39 — Magnetic commissioning request specification

The provider shall answer each item explicitly.

## Measurand and geometry

1. Can you calibrate magnetic flux density or field at the final sample position?
2. Can the calibration include the actual cryostat/sample holder geometry or an uncertainty-bearing transfer?
3. What range and point density are available near all reversal regions?
4. Can the service cover both sweep directions?

## Traceability

5. Accreditation or national-metrology scope.
6. Certificate identifiers and traceability chain.
7. Reference instrument, serial and validity.
8. Calibration method and environmental conditions.

## Required campaign

9. Pre-acquisition ascending and descending sweeps.
10. Post-acquisition ascending and descending sweeps.
11. Command, readback and reference field at every calibration point.
12. Repeat measurements sufficient to quantify repeatability.
13. Positioning sensitivity and field-gradient map.
14. Hysteresis and drift assessment.
15. Full-range bracketing.

## Deliverables

16. Raw calibration data.
17. Measurement model.
18. uncertainty budget and coverage factor.
19. covariance or information sufficient to reconstruct covariance.
20. signed scope confirmation.
21. machine-readable receipt and artifact hashes.

## Non-negotiable boundary

A catalogue accuracy, calibration of the probe alone, or an accreditation number without final-system transfer is insufficient.
