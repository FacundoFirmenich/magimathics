# HGMF‑HG39 — Provider and method route

## Magnetic priority order

### Route M1 — accredited Spanish magnetic laboratory

Primary candidate: UPM Laboratorio de Medidas Magnéticas, ENAC 17/LC10.010.

Required service confirmation must explicitly state:

- calibration of the exact Hall probe/reference chain;
- full range and dense reversal-region coverage;
- ascending and descending sweeps;
- pre/post calibration;
- uncertainty and covariance or sufficient raw calibration data;
- transfer or validation at the final sample location/geometry;
- certificate under accredited scope where applicable.

### Route M2 — national-metrology route

CEM MAGLAB is the preferred national-metrology escalation route. A service confirmation must establish that the requested range, uncertainty and geometry are currently available.

### Route M3 — international backup

NPL provides calibration/validation for Hall gaussmeters, fluxgates, coils and bespoke/on-site magnetic systems.

### Local screening

An ENAC-listed provider in Ripollet may reduce logistics, but its exact accredited scope and uncertainty must be obtained before selection.

## Adsorption priority order

### Route A1 — custom Xe reference campaign

A local BET/porosimetry facility is useful only if it confirms in writing that it can execute:

- Xe at the preregistered temperature;
- a matched nonporous silica reference;
- same gas lot and pretreatment;
- interleaved reference/sample/reference measurements;
- traceable pressure, temperature and mass;
- raw data and full covariance.

Routine N2 BET service is not equivalent.

### Route A2 — reference specimen

A certified surface-area material may validate the instrument. The scientific `Vs` reference must still be a matched nonporous silica specimen.

## Topology priority order

### Route T1 — SAXS/USAXS

ALBA NCD-SWEET is a local SAXS/WAXS route for porous materials. Absolute-intensity calibration and an uncertainty-bearing model/set output are required.

### Route T2 — connectivity

Connectivity needs a 3D or connectivity-sensitive method whose resolution is demonstrated to be adequate for the pore/throat scale. TEM tomography or an advanced nano-tomography route may be used; ordinary micro-CT is not automatically adequate.

### Combined receipt

The topology receipt should combine:

- size/surface information;
- connectivity constraint;
- resolution study;
- cross-method agreement or conservative set intersection.

No single provider website or instrument specification grants arming authority.
