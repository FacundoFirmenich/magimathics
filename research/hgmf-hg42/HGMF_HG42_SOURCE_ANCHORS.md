# HGMF‑HG42 — Source anchors

## Internal canonical inputs

- HG41: quotient measure, historical support-only ceiling and HG42 protocol.
- HG40: affine quotient and adsorption order diagnostic.

## Statistical anchors

- Wasserman, Ramdas and Balakrishnan, “Universal Inference”, PNAS 2020: split likelihood ratios with finite-sample validity for composite and irregular nulls, including nuisance profiling and likelihood upper bounds.
- Grünwald, de Heide and Koolen, “Safe Testing”, JRSSB 2024: e-values, composite nulls, nuisance parameters and safe optional continuation.
- Wijsman, invariant measures and maximal invariants: induced distributions under group actions.
- Saha and Ramdas, robust likelihood-ratio e-processes for composite nulls: conditional e-factor and sequential validity architecture.

These sources anchor the statistical machinery. They do not establish carrier physics, a real quotient noise law, an empirical HGMF effect or physical protogeometry.
