# P2 / P2b Editorial Coordination Contract

## Shared programme

Both manuscripts use the operational definition:

\[
\mathrm{UMI}_{\mathcal J}(C)
\iff
\operatorname{Closed}(C)
\land
\operatorname{Sufficient}_{\mathcal J}(C)
\land
\operatorname{Minimal}_{\subseteq,\mathcal J}(C)
\land
\operatorname{Invariant}_{\mathcal J}(C).
\]

A UMI is an inclusion-minimal relational mechanism class that preserves a frozen semantic signature under admissible substitution and transport, and loses that signature under critical ablation or rewiring.

## P2 — general systems/methods paper

**Question:** Can one frozen intervention tribunal identify relational units across systems with different materials, equations, scales and functions?

**Primary contribution:** the tribunal itself, its formal decision rule, the cross-domain portfolio, designed and naturalistic decoys, semantic-signature sensitivity, abstention and the refinement relation among UMI classes.

**Primary cases:** all domains, represented compactly. Biological cases are cross-domain instances rather than detailed biological narratives.

**Primary figures:** tribunal architecture; cross-domain carrier matrix; benchmark selectivity; semantic sensitivity; refinement lattice.

**Preprint home:** arXiv, primarily `nlin.AO` or `cs.SY`.

## P2b — synthetic and systems biology paper

**Question:** Can relational biological units be identified experimentally and mechanistically beyond structural motif occurrence or phenotype resemblance?

**Primary contribution:** biological evidence architecture and detailed application to oscillation, robust adaptation, bistable memory, kinetic proofreading, synthetic Turing patterning and autocatalytic closure, with quorum-sensing hybrids as an abstention case.

**Primary figures:** biological evidence ladder; repressilator interventions; RPA transport; memory/proofreading/patterning mechanism–decoy comparisons; biological carrier hierarchy.

**Preprint home:** bioRxiv, primarily `Systems Biology`, with `Synthetic Biology` as alternative.

## Non-overlap rules

1. P2 owns the general formal tribunal and its aggregate performance claims.
2. P2b owns the biological mechanistic depth and domain-specific intervention synthesis.
3. P2 reports biological cases in one compact comparative table; P2b expands them with biological context, kinetic jurisdiction and primary experimental evidence.
4. P2 owns cross-domain non-biological cases: error correction, consensus safety, topological transport and mechanical/electronic homeostasis.
5. P2b may cite cross-substrate electronic implementations only as transport controls, not as additional biological case studies.
6. The blinded benchmark confusion matrices belong to P2. P2b may report only the biology-relevant decoy pairs and the quorum-sensing abstention.
7. Neither paper claims discovery of the underlying established mechanisms.
8. Neither paper promotes empirical multifractality or a new ontology.
9. Each paper must cite the other once both have stable public identifiers.

## Shared release architecture

- manuscript source: GitHub;
- software release: Zenodo DOI;
- frozen result tables and receipts: Zenodo dataset DOI when distinct from software;
- preprint: arXiv for P2, bioRxiv for P2b;
- `CITATION.cff`, provenance manifest and claim ledger required before v1 submission.
