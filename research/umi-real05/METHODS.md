# MGM‑UMI‑REAL‑05 — Frozen Methods

A case is classified `POSITIVE` only when all conditions hold:

1. the strict semantic signature is present;
2. at least two independent substitutions preserve it;
3. critical ablation destroys it;
4. a close rewire or phenotypic decoy fails it;
5. the mechanism transports across at least two implementations;
6. one minimal relational mechanism class remains.

Observed hard failure gives `NEGATIVE`. Incomplete evidence without hard failure gives `ABSTAIN`.

## Evidence separation

- `T1`: exact theorem or exhaustive finite check;
- `P1`: primary direct experiment;
- `P2`: primary perturbation/ablation;
- `X1`: cross-substrate realization;
- `A`: ambiguous or incomplete evidence.

No numerical synthetic dataset is used as empirical evidence. Low-dimensional models appear only as exact theorem controls.
