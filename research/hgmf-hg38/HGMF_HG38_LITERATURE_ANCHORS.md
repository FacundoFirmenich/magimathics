# HGMF‑HG38 — Literature and standards anchors

## Metrology

- JCGM 100:2008, *Guide to the Expression of Uncertainty in Measurement*: general rules for evaluating and expressing measurement uncertainty, including calibration and basic research.
- JCGM GUM-6:2020: development and use of measurement models.
- JCGM 106:2012: role of uncertainty and decision rules in conformity assessment.
- JCGM 200:2012, VIM: metrological traceability is a property of a measurement result related to a reference through a documented unbroken chain of calibrations, each contributing to uncertainty.
- NIST Technical Note 2156 and NIST traceability policy: traceability alone does not guarantee fitness for purpose; the provider must document the complete system and uncertainty.

## Magnetic calibration

- NIST magnetic-field measurement literature emphasizes probe geometry, calibration and uncertainty; Hall probes require temperature-aware calibration and are not intrinsically sufficient merely by being present.

## Adsorption

- IUPAC physisorption technical guidance emphasizes authoritative methodology, experimental protocol, surface area and pore-size characterization, and the limits of inference from adsorption data.
- The prospective cartridge requires an independent film-reference result rather than selecting `Vs` from the porous holdout data.

These sources govern measurement validity and protocol design. They do not supply prospective outcomes or establish HGMF.
