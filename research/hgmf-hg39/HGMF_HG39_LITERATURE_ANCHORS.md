# HGMF‑HG39 — Literature and capability anchors

## Metrology

- BIPM/JCGM publications: GUM, GUM‑6 and JCGM 106.
- ISO/IEC 17025:2017: competence, impartiality and consistent operation of testing/calibration laboratories.
- ILAC P10: metrological traceability policy.
- NIST traceability policy: traceability is a property of the measurement result; use of a calibrated instrument alone is insufficient.

## Magnetic commissioning

- UPM Laboratorio de Medidas Magnéticas: ENAC 17/LC10.010, magnetic calibration, Hall gaussmeters, electromagnets/Helmholtz coils and NMR reference capability.
- CEM MAGLAB/TRaMM: national Spanish magnetic standards and calibration capability.
- NPL Magnetics: accredited calibration/validation of Hall gaussmeters, fluxgates, coils and bespoke/on-site systems.
- NIST magnetic sensing/metrology: NMR-based calibration over part of the field range and transfer through Hall probes.

## Adsorption and porosity

- ISO 9277:2022: BET specific surface area.
- ISO 15901‑2:2022: nanopore analysis by gas adsorption.
- IUPAC 2015 physisorption technical report.
- NIST SRM 1897/2207: specific-surface-area/controlled-pore-glass standards for instrument performance.

## Independent topology

- ISO 17867:2020: SAXS particle-size analysis.
- ISO 20804:2022: specific surface area by SAXS.
- NIST SRM 3600: absolute SAXS intensity calibration.
- ALBA NCD-SWEET: SAXS/WAXS capability for porous and mesoporous materials.
- Advanced 3D microscopy/tomography remains necessary for connectivity unless a conservative connectivity set is independently justified.

These anchors define commissioning routes; none is itself a completed HGMF receipt.
