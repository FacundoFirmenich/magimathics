# HGMF‑HG01 — Non-Temporal Generative Hierarchy and Projection Semantics

## Objective

Open Paper C formally without treating Paper B as evidence for the foundational programme.

## Gate A — founding order

Formalize non-temporal priority:

\[
\mathsf{PreGeom}\succ_{fund}\mathsf{ProtoGeom}\rightsquigarrow\mathsf{Geom}.
\]

## Gate B — pregeometry

Define only its founding position and negative intelligibility conditions. Do not assign points, relations, fields, time or morphisms in advance.

## Gate C — protogeometry

Formalize a relational multifractal code of realizability:

- relations and relations of relations;
- categorical and conjunctive groupings;
- nesting, subnesting and supernesting;
- restrictions and admissible realizations;
- non-psychological teleological orientation.

## Gate D — geometry

Define projection and stabilization:

\[
G=\operatorname{Stab}[\Pi(P)].
\]

## Gate E — non-invertibility

Determine when multiple protogeometric configurations generate the same geometry and which invariants survive.

## Gate F — hypergeometry

Formalize it as the explanatory grammar shared by pregeometry, protogeometry and geometry, not as a containing super-space.

## Gate G — constants boundary

Do not introduce \(\pi/6\), \(\pi/18\), \(4\pi/9\) or RMNC until an independent and unique selection theorem is available.
