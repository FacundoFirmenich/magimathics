# HGMF‑UMI‑19 — Scale-Indexed Semantic Measure and Multifractal Interrelation

## Objective

Close the second expansion of UMI: `Unidad Multifractal de Interrelación`.

## Gate A — semantic measure

Define a nonnegative measure over enabled realizations, restrictions, intervention sensitivity, causal contribution, memory and composition.

## Gate B — scale and covers

Freeze a family of grains/covers \(\mathcal G_\ell\) before seeing the spectrum.

## Gate C — transport

Determine which quantities survive changes of carrier, operator, gauge and scale.

## Gate D — spectrum

Construct

\[
Z(q,\ell),\quad \tau(q),\quad D_q,\quad f(\alpha).
\]

## Gate E — no-go tribunal

Separate genuine multifractality from finite mixtures, heteroscedasticity, regime changes and aggregation artifacts.

## Gate F — triaxiality

Audit vertical, horizontal and diagonal components separately and jointly.

## Gate G — dual acronym

Adjudicate whether

\[
\text{Unidad Mínima Invariante}\equiv\text{Unidad Multifractal de Interrelación}
\]

is a theorem, an additional condition or only a partial overlap.

- `PASS_MULTIFRACTAL_UMI_CHARACTERIZATION`
- `PASS_MULTISCALE_ONLY`
- `FAIL_MULTIFRACTALITY`
