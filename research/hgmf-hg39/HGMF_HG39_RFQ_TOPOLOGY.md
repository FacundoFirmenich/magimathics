# HGMF‑HG39 — Independent topology commissioning request specification

## Sample and independence

1. Characterize a preregistered sister specimen or split with documented ancestry.
2. Complete characterization before holdout.
3. Confirm no holdout outcomes are used in processing or model selection.

## Required information

4. pore-size distribution or conservative set;
5. specific surface or interface density;
6. connectivity/coordination constraint;
7. porosity;
8. resolution and point-spread function;
9. segmentation/model uncertainty;
10. sample heterogeneity and split uncertainty.

## Preferred combined route

- absolute-intensity SAXS/USAXS for size/surface;
- 3D electron or X-ray tomography for connectivity where resolution is adequate;
- conservative set-valued integration if methods disagree.

## Required deliverables

- calibrated raw data;
- calibration/reference material IDs;
- processing code and environment;
- uncertainty/covariance or admissible set;
- resolution-adequacy report;
- machine-readable topology receipt and hashes.
