# HGMF‑HG34 — Structural carrier falsifiers and physical successor nulls

## Objetivo

Cerrar las dos piezas que un unconstrained single-path Preisach fit no puede resolver:

1. falsadores estructurales cross-history;
2. physical carrier-specific successor models.

## Gate A — adsorption Everett tribunal

Sobre los runs reales de Xe/Vycor:

- return-point memory;
- wiping-out;
- congruency;
- closure/error at repeated reversal points;
- Everett-function consistency where identifiable.

Los tests deben preservar uncertainty y whole-run ancestry.

## Gate B — adsorption network null

Implementar un model family que represente, según jurisdicción:

- percolation/connectivity;
- pore blocking;
- cavitation;
- capillary condensation.

No se autoriza una surrogate family sin relación con Vycor/Xe.

## Gate C — magnetic training/domain-state tribunal

Usar el source paper para congelar predicciones estructurales:

- first loop after +2.5 T preset differs from subsequent loops;
- abrupt reversal on one side vs domain-mediated gradual reversal on the opposite side;
- disappearance/reduction of exchange bias after training;
- history-dependent domain evolution.

## Gate D — magnetic observation bridge

Definir cómo un source-specific domain-state/micromagnetic prediction induce una distribution sobre AHE \(V_{xy}\) o sobre components extraídos.

## Gate E — Preisach role

Preisach remains a phenomenological control. It cannot be used as the sole physical standard null.

## Gate F — e-value integration

Any physical successor model entering X must deliver:

- likelihood;
- certified likelihood upper bound;
- or another finite-sample e-value.
