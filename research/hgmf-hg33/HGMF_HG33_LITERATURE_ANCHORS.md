# HGMF‑HG33 — Literature anchors

## Magnetic source

- Kumar et al., Small 21, e06284 (2025), DOI 10.1002/smll.202506284.
  Source-specific mechanism: interfacial exchange coupling, orthogonal anisotropies, stripe/flux-closure domains, domain nucleation/propagation, canted CrSBr magnetization and training effect.

## Magnetic phenomenological hysteresis

- Classical Preisach literature: scalar hysteresis represented as an integral/superposition of relay hysterons over a threshold plane.
- Jiles–Atherton and Preisach remain standard macroscopic magnetic hysteresis model families, but neither is automatically the microscopic model of FGT/CrSBr exchange bias.

## Adsorption source

- Siderius, Journal of Research of NIST 126, 126037 (2021), DOI 10.6028/jres.126.037.
- NIST dataset DOI 10.18434/mds2-2466.
- Brown scanning isotherms: Xe on Vycor around 151 K.

## Adsorption theory

- The NIST digitization paper states that Brown's scanning data were used to test independent domain theory.
- Independent-domain theory treats pores/domains as binary filled/empty units with condensation and evaporation pressures, making the binary core Preisach-like.
- Modern scanning-isotherm work on Vycor includes percolation/pore-network descriptions with pore blocking and connectivity.
- Recent pore-network approaches couple percolation with DFT kernels and explicitly model condensation, evaporation, pore blocking and cavitation.

## Claim boundary

These references establish the appropriate standard competitor families. They do not establish HGMF or rule out alternative carrier physics.
