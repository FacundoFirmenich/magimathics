# HGMF‑UMI‑22 — Native Intervention Hierarchies and Independent Scale Extension

## Objective

Replace sequence-order proxies by explicit intervention/reset hierarchies and at least eight independent physical or replicated scales per carrier.

## Requirements

1. Two non-isomorphic carriers.
2. Native vertical witness from actual interventions or reset transitions.
3. At least eight independent levels; interpolation or repeated rebinning may not substitute for scale.
4. Full, regime, block, hierarchy and finite-mixture surrogate tribunals.
5. A semantic transport map and equivalence margin frozen before outcomes.
6. Marginal and joint H/D/V spectra plus a coupling test.
7. `ABSTAIN` when scale range or intervention depth is insufficient.

## Portfolio contract

- Paper A remains governed by the UMI‑17 experiment.
- Paper B may claim formal characterization and empirical native multiscale organization, not universal empirical multifractality.
- Paper C receives no bridge unless native spectrum, surrogates and transport all pass.
