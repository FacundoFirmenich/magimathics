# HGMF‑HG38 — Metrology and calibration contract

## Governing principles

The cartridge adopts:

- JCGM 100:2008 for uncertainty evaluation and expression;
- JCGM GUM-6:2020 for measurement-model construction;
- JCGM 106:2012 for decision rules and conformity assessment;
- JCGM 200:2012/VIM for metrological traceability;
- NIST traceability policy for the requirement that the **measurement result**, not merely the instrument, be linked through a documented calibration chain.

## Magnetic result

A valid field result must include:

1. measurand definition at the sample location;
2. command/readback/reference map;
3. traceability chain;
4. calibration validity interval;
5. full uncertainty budget;
6. sweep-direction and hysteresis diagnostics;
7. pre/post drift;
8. covariance across experimental field points;
9. fitness-for-purpose decision rule.

A calibration certificate alone is insufficient if the complete measurement system, geometry and environment are not represented.

## Adsorption result

A valid adsorption reference result must include:

1. Xe identity, lot and purity;
2. reference and sample surface-chemistry/pretreatment relation;
3. mass and surface-area results with uncertainty;
4. traceable temperature and pressure results;
5. equilibrium criterion;
6. `Vs` and `Vc` over the entire pressure domain;
7. covariance across the reference curves;
8. interleaved reference/sample order;
9. independent topology characterization.

## Fitness for purpose

Traceability alone is not enough. The uncertainty must be small enough for the preregistered decision.

HG38 therefore refuses to arm when a real receipt lacks an explicit numeric fitness limit or fails it.
