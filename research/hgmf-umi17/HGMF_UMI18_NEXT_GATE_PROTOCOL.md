# HGMF‑UMI‑18 — Representation-Independent UMI Characterization

Paper A is now experiment-ready. Until the bridge is physically executed, the active gate advances Paper B.

## Objective

Define necessary and sufficient conditions for two systems to instantiate the same UMI independently of carrier, operator, scale and coordinate representation.

## Gate A — jurisdiction

Specify admissible observations, interventions, transports, tolerated loss, scale and grain.

## Gate B — semantic signature

Construct the signature from enabled and forbidden realizations, intervention responses, history dependence and closure conditions.

## Gate C — equivalence

Prove reflexivity, symmetry and transitivity, or identify exactly why only a preorder or tolerance relation is available.

## Gate D — minimality

Separate inclusion, causal, representational and scale-relative minimality.

## Gate E — composition

Determine when UMIs compose into another UMI and when composition destroys invariance.

## Gate F — antiaggregation

Prove which summaries are sufficient and which collapse distinct semantic classes.

## Gate G — counterexamples

Construct:

- same carrier, different UMI;
- different carriers, same UMI;
- same operator, different semantics;
- same semantics, different operators.

## Authorized outcomes

- `PASS_UMI_CHARACTERIZATION_THEOREM`
- `PASS_PREORDER_ONLY`
- `FAIL_TRANSITIVITY`
- `FAIL_MINIMALITY_UNIQUENESS`
- `HOLD_SEMANTIC_SIGNATURE`
